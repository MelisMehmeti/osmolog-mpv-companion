# Osmolog branding

The MIT License applies to the companion source code. It does not grant rights
to the Osmolog name, logo, product identity, or the branding files under
"assets/".

Those brand assets are included so official builds can be reproduced and
audited. Modified or redistributed builds must remove Osmolog branding unless
the Osmolog owner has given separate written permission. Nothing in this
repository implies endorsement of a third-party build.

Third-party player icons are included only to identify supported applications:

- `assets/mpv.svg`: the existing Osmolog source icon, optimized from MPV's
  [official application icon](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/blob/master/etc/mpv-gradient.svg).
- `assets/manatan.png`: Manatan's [official website app icon](https://manatan.com/assets/apple-touch-icon.png).
- `assets/steam.svg`: the existing Osmolog Steam source icon; Steam and its logo belong to Valve Corporation.

These player names and logos belong to their respective owners, not Osmolog.
